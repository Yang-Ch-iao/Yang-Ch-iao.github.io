01 / LLM AGENTS & RETRIEVAL

Reasoning over evolving enterprise knowledge

EnterpriseSaga builds a coherent company history across 12 enterprise systems. Questions require agents to connect evidence across systems and time, while distinguishing relevant events from similar activities.

I led the benchmark from company simulation and question construction to retrieval pipelines and agent evaluation: 1.213 billion tokens and 966 evidence-grounded questions.

02 / AI FOR SCIENCE

World models for molecular optimization

MolWorld introduces molecule-transfer graphs: a growing network of whole molecules linked by matched molecular pair (MMP) relations. A world model predicts local graph expansion to guide generation, and verified MMP links keep new candidates connected to the starting series.

Research collaboration with Merck & Co., Inc.

03 / MULTIMODAL LEARNING

Representations that depend on the relation

RCML uses natural-language relations to reshape multimodal representations. Shared image and text features support multiple relation-conditioned spaces, so relevance can change with the relation being queried.

Complementary relation-to-pair and pair-to-relation contrastive objectives learn compatibility within each relation and distinguish it from other relations.

Related work: MILNet · multimodal sarcasm detection

AI FOR NEUROSCIENCE

XAI-Refine

An explanation–knowledge loop that checks brain-age model explanations against neuroscience literature, converts supported evidence into targeted constraints, and validates each model revision.

With Emory Radiology and Neurology.

Paper ↗
HUMAN BEHAVIOR MODELING

Profile-conditioned latent dynamics

I proposed a world-model formulation that separates stable psychometric traits from evolving behavioral states. Observed transitions supervise latent dynamics during training; a profile-conditioned prior predicts transitions at inference.

With Emory Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences.
