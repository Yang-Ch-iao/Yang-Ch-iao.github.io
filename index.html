01 / LLM AGENTS & RETRIEVAL

Reasoning over evolving enterprise knowledge

EnterpriseSaga tests how agents retrieve evidence and reason across systems and time. I led its development: a 1.213-billion-token synthetic enterprise benchmark with 966 evidence-grounded questions and seven retrieval pipelines and agents.

02 / AI FOR SCIENCE

World models for molecular optimization

MolWorld introduces molecule-transfer graphs for sequential molecular optimization. A world model predicts local graph expansion to guide generation, while verified matched molecular pair relations keep new candidates connected to known molecules.

Research collaboration with Merck & Co., Inc.

03 / MULTIMODAL LEARNING

Representations that depend on the relation

RCML conditions multimodal representations on natural-language relations, so the same sample can express different aspects of relevance. Its contrastive objective connects image–text alignment with many-to-many relations across samples.

Related work: MILNet · multimodal sarcasm detection
