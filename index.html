02

AI for Science

MOLECULAR OPTIMIZATION

World models for molecular optimization

MolWorld introduces molecule-transfer graphs: a growing network of whole molecules linked by matched molecular pair (MMP) relations. A world model guides the selection of connected five-molecule anchor contexts. Each context conditions multiple candidates, which typically link to most or all five anchors and may also connect to other molecules. MMP verification determines which candidates and edges join the graph.

Research collaboration with Merck & Co., Inc.

AI FOR NEUROSCIENCE

XAI-Refine

Accepted model updates feed the next round of explanation discovery.

A closed explanation–knowledge loop for brain-age prediction: reliable explanations are checked against literature, minimally revised, and converted into constraints. Updates that pass predictive and explanatory checks are re-analyzed in the next iteration.

With Emory Radiology and Neurology.

HUMAN BEHAVIOR MODELING

Profile-conditioned latent dynamics

I proposed a world-model formulation that separates stable psychometric traits from evolving behavioral states. Observed transitions supervise latent dynamics during training; a profile-conditioned prior predicts transitions at inference.

With Emory Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences.

SEMANTIC RELATIONS

Relation-conditioned multimodal embeddings

RCML maps the same multimodal sample to different embeddings for different natural-language relations. Shared image and text features are reused; relation-conditioned projections and contrastive objectives change which samples are close in each space.

MULTIMODAL SARCASM DETECTION · AAAI 2023

MILNet

MILNet couples local semantic-guided and global incongruity learning. Object relations reveal local conflicts, while whole-image context captures what object-level features can miss. The branches reinforce each other through screened, bidirectional knowledge transfer.
